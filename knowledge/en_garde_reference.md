# En garde and basic footwork: teaching reference

The Northwest Fencing Center beginner guide (p.3) teaches feet approximately perpendicular,
knees aligned over their toes, and a stance that supports balance and mobility. Advance begins
with the front foot; retreat with the rear foot. Use these as beginner drill cues. A side view
cannot establish whether a knee tracks inward relative to the toes; ask for a front view for
that question. No universal en-garde knee angle is established by this guide.

Source: https://www.nwfencing.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Beginner-Guide.pdf
