# Fleche versus lunge: epee research reference

Bober et al. studied two national-team male epee fencers. Resultant body center-of-gravity
velocities were 2.64 and 2.89 m/s for fleche, versus 1.94 and 2.21 m/s for lunge.
These are case observations, not a standard or individual speed target. They support treating
fleche as a separate movement. Current hip-proxy speed and torso/s do not match this measure.
Use event sequence, intended touch and movement-specific comparisons rather than a lunge
endpoint template to assess a fleche.

Source: https://doi.org/10.1515/pjst-2016-0023
