# Fleche movement time reference

Borysiuk et al. (2019), studying six world-leading female epee fencers, report mean movement
times of 510 ms for fleche and 568 ms for lunge in coach-triggered attacks. Reaction time
is separate; attacks ended at successful touch. The paper's discussion clarifies milliseconds
despite an inconsistent decimal table heading. These means are not beginner targets and
cannot grade Flèche onset-to-peak or recovery durations. Match movement events first.

Source: https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph16132315
