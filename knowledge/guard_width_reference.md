# En garde stance width: individual reference

Akbas et al. (2023) observed preferred stance width of 40.91 +/- 5.92 cm in eight elite female
epee fencers. Width was measured from front heel to rear-foot inner edge, averaged over three
natural stances. This is a small cohort description, not a universal 41 cm prescription.
Flèche's ankle-center stance/torso ratio uses different landmarks and units. Establish
the athlete's own repeatable, usable en-garde stance before comparing changes.

Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10203838/
