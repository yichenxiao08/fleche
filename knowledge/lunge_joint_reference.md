# Lunge knee-angle research reference

A 12-man skilled foil study reports front-knee peak flexion of 74.00 +/- 13.13 degrees for
a stationary lunge and 92.00 +/- 12.27 for an advance-lunge at its normal target distance
(1.5 times height). These are 3D anatomical flexion peaks over a phase, not ideal angles.
Flèche measures a 2D included angle at a selected frame. Direct numerical grading is
invalid without matching convention, phase, distance and camera geometry; SD is not a
correctness tolerance. Movement type matters when interpreting knee bend.

Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13050851/
