# Lunge speed reference: blade versus body

Mulloy et al. (2018) reported sword velocity of 3.24 +/- 0.24 m/s in seven skilled fencers
and 2.69 +/- 0.29 m/s in eight novices. This small foil-lunge study links performance to
rear-leg hip-knee-ankle coordination. These group summaries are not individual good/bad
cutoffs. FenceCoach's normalized recovery hip speed cannot be compared to sword velocity.
Use the finding to investigate coordinated propulsion, not to prescribe a universal speed.

Source: https://doi.org/10.1080/23335432.2018.1454342
