# Simple lunge: a teaching model

The NWFC beginner guide (p.3) teaches arm extension toward the target with rear-leg push,
followed by a finish with the front knee bent and rear leg extended. Its illustration uses
a right-angle front leg. This is a conventional beginner endpoint, not a validated 90-degree
pass/fail rule for every lunge. Compare the intended action and actual finish before giving
a cue. Investigate rear-leg drive, arm/foot sequence, landing and recovery together.

Source: https://www.nwfencing.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Beginner-Guide.pdf
