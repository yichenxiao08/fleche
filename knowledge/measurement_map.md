# Measurement map

All readings are 2D projections at a named phase, with declared sides/facing. Rejected/unsupported tracking is withheld. Match qualitative KB cues only: no numerical pass/fail cutoffs. Near 180 degrees is geometrically near-straight, not a target. A finish reading cannot establish earlier sequencing. Bigger/smaller is not automatically better/worse.

| Measurement | Meaning/sign; KB connection | Limits |
|---|---|---|
| front_knee_angle | Hip-knee-ankle included angle: smaller more bent, near 180 straighter. KB 2-4: bent guard/finish, extension during travel. | No angle target; not knee/ankle alignment, inward collapse, load or safety. |
| rear_knee_angle | Larger straighter. KB 3: near-straight finish fits straight-rear-leg cue; bent finish misses it. KB 5: bends on recovery. | Not force, push direction, foot contact or locking. |
| weapon_elbow_angle | Shoulder-elbow-wrist included angle, larger straighter. KB 3: extension at measured phase. | Not locking, relaxed shoulder, point aim or arm-first timing. |
| weapon_arm_direction | Shoulder-wrist inclination: positive wrist above shoulder, negative below; horizontal displacement absolute. KB 3: toward target. | Not shoulder height, elbow bend, toward/away direction or blade aim; no target angle. |
| projected_torso_tilt | Nonnegative magnitude from image vertical; smaller more upright, no direction. KB 3: upright/slightly forward. | Not spinal shape, balance or strength. REVIEW: slight/heavy undefined; no degree boundary. |
| forward_torso_pitch_change | Facing-corrected change from guard: positive more forward, negative less. KB 3/6: relative posture change. | Needs usable guard/facing; not absolute lean, spinal shape or cause. |
| front_knee_ahead_of_toe | (knee x - toe x) x facing sign / torso pixels. Facing right +1, left -1. POSITIVE beyond toe toward target, NEGATIVE behind, ZERO same forward position. KB 3/6: knee-past-toe cue. | Behind toe does not prove over-ankle alignment. Not inward collapse, force or injury. REVIEW: near-zero perspective/landmark uncertainty; no tolerance invented. |
| action_to_lunge_time | Seconds from action anchor to lunge bookmark. KB 3/11: like-defined interval comparison. | No time target; not reaction/touch time, arm-first order or whole-attack speed. |
| estimated_active_recovery_time | Seconds from backward hip-motion onset to guard return, EXCLUDES hold. KB 5: relative duration. | Not onset timestamp, arm-last order, balance or readiness; no target. |
| finish_to_guard_time | Seconds from finish to guard return, INCLUDES hold. KB 5: total interval. | Not active recovery alone, hesitation or quality; no target. |
| action_projected_hip_speed | Projected hip speed / initial torso length, torso lengths/second. KB 1/3/11: comparable travel. | Not m/s, sword speed, force or power; no target. REVIEW: direction needs producer/facing; magnitude alone gives none. |

Other readings: arm-extension ratio = shoulder-wrist reach / summed upper-arm/forearm length; nearer one suggests straighter projected arm (KB 3), not aim/timing. Raw events differ from phase medians. Non-weapon angles describe only that arm (KB 4). Feet separation describes spacing (KB 2), not an ideal stance.

No matching numerical criteria for angles, speeds, intervals, distance, spread/consistency, force or power. Describe ranges, not the best rep. KB drill counts are instructions, not athlete readings.

Fixed side-view pose footage cannot assess inward knee collapse, rear-foot roll/contact or point drop/blade control. Do not suggest another angle unless the KB does. No shoulder tension, tactical effects, injury or arm-last order is measured. KB 8 has almost no fleche/advance/retreat teaching: acknowledge that and use only general principles actually written there, never invented mechanics/drills or a verdict from lunge data.
