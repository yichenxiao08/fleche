# Lunge rear-leg drive

Guan et al. compared seven elite and twelve intermediate fencers performing maximum-effort
lunges. Faster elite body motion accompanied higher rear-leg ground force, with rear-knee
power, moment and range of motion associated with performance. This supports investigating
rear-leg propulsion when a lunge seems slow. It does not establish a single ideal knee bend
or show that an athlete has weak legs from a pose angle. Compare the same action and distance;
explain rear-leg drive as a possible focus, then check the movement sequence.

Source: https://doi.org/10.1080/17461391.2017.1414886
