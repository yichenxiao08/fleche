# Recovery: returning from a lunge to en garde

Recovery in a basic lunge drill means returning from the extended lunge position to
en garde, ready for the next action. Recovering back brings the fencer back toward
the starting guard position. It is a return phase, not another attack or a vague
transition from offense to defense. In the NWFC beginner teaching sequence,
recovering back begins by releasing the rear knee and pushing from the front leg.
Describe the return clearly before discussing measured duration.

Source: Northwest Fencing Center beginner guide, page 3, "Recover back":
https://www.nwfencing.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Beginner-Guide.pdf

This is drill vocabulary, not proof of an athlete's balance or a speed target.
