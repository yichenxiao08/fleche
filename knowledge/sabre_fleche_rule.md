# Sabre and the fleche

FIE Technical Rules, August 2026, t.101.5 forbid the fleche and forward movements where the
rear foot completely passes the front foot in sabre. Do not give a sabre fencer an epee
crossing-step fleche drill. Research measurements from epee fleches are not sabre movement
baselines. Identify the weapon and intended action before recommending the exercise.
